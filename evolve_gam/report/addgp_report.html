The idea

Every component is one-dimensional, so the data collapses

A GA2M predicts with main effects and pairwise terms only: y = Σ fj(xj) + Σ fab(xa,xb) + ε. Fitting that as a Gaussian process normally means an n×n kernel matrix — hopeless past a few thousand rows.

But quantile-bin each feature first, and every component becomes a function on a small grid: one value per bin. Write Z for the indicator matrix that says which bin each row falls in. Then the exact GP marginal likelihood touches the data only through three quantities:

C = ZTZ  (bin co-occurrence counts)  ·  b = ZTy  (bin sums)  ·  yTy

Those are P×P and P numbers, where P is the total bin count — a few thousand, fixed by a budget. One pass over the data builds them; every optimizer step afterwards works on them alone. The fit is O(P³) per step and completely independent of n.

The prior

Two kernels per feature, and the likelihood picks the mix

Each feature's shape function gets a GP prior built from exactly two covariance kernels on its bin grid: a Matérn-½ at lengthscale 0.05, which produces rough, jumpy functions, and a squared-exponential at 0.25, which produces smooth ones. Their amplitudes are free parameters. Maximizing the marginal likelihood chooses the blend per feature — so smoothness is inferred, never tuned, and a feature that needs a step function and a feature that needs a gentle curve are both served without a lengthscale search.

The same amplitudes do feature selection. A feature that explains nothing has its amplitude driven toward zero and drops out of the model — automatic relevance determination, for free, as a by-product of the same objective.

The fit

What happens when you call fit

  1. Condition the target

    If the target is positive and taking logs substantially reduces its skew, fit on the log scale. Winsorize extreme outliers at a guarded Tukey fence — only when the fence would clip at most 1% of rows, so zero-inflated targets are left alone.

  2. Bin every feature

    Quantile bins, with the bin count set by a parameter budget divided across features. Capacity is a schedule in n: below 1,000 rows a lean 64-bin model, above it a 256-bin one.

  3. Reduce to sufficient statistics

    One pass builds C, b, yᵀy. The rows are never touched again.

  4. Fit main effects by marginal likelihood

    Gradient ascent on the log-amplitudes and the noise level — the exact GP evidence, with no priors and no penalty. The finite step count is the only regularizer, and it is a deliberate one.

  5. Screen candidate pairs

    FAST screening on the residual: shrunken cell means over coarse 2-D grids rank which interactions are worth spending parameters on.

  6. Fit pair terms blockwise-jointly

    Pairs are fit in chunks that share shrinkage, each chunk trying several grid resolutions and keeping whichever the marginal likelihood prefers, alternating twice with a refit of the main effects.

  7. Read out

    Every component becomes a lookup table over bin midpoints, interpolated at predict time. The model you inspect is exactly the model that predicts.

No randomness anywhere. No cross-validation, no train/validation split, no bagging, no seeds. Two runs on the same data give the same model; every structural choice — smoothness, relevance, which interactions to include, how finely to grid them — is made by the same marginal likelihood.

Evidence

Four benchmark suites

Two suites were used during development; two were held out. CTR23 in particular was downloaded only after the method was finished, and no design decision was ever made against it.

Method development

What was taken out, and what fought back

The model began as a 1,110-line pipeline with a boosted tree ensemble bolted on. Thirteen rounds of ablation removed everything that could not prove its worth. Limits

Where it loses, and why that is the same answer every time

The losses are consistent across all four suites and they are not fitting failures. They are the model class: smooth deterministic simulations and heavy-interaction data, where the target depends on three or more inputs jointly and no sum of pairwise pieces can represent it.

On CTR23 the worst cases are wave energy (+109% vs EBM), building energy efficiency (+79%), an FPS benchmark (+39%), FIFA player ratings (+34%) and robot arm dynamics (+17%) — physics simulations and sports ratings. On TabArena it is molecular fingerprints and superconductor critical temperature, where even EBM trails random forests. A model restricted to main effects and pairs has a ceiling on that data, and this one sits at it.

Against the black-box foundation model TabPFN the pattern is equally consistent: TabPFN leads three of four suites, AddGP leads the interpretable field on all four. On TabArena, where several datasets exceed TabPFN's memory limits, AddGP finishes first overall.

All figures from a single 80/20 split per dataset at random_state=42, RMSE on the train-standardized target, models at library defaults. This is defaults-versus-defaults, not a tuned comparison; TabArena and CTR23 both define richer official protocols with repeated folds and hyperparameter search.
